## Rules

- Every study starts with a component board. No board, no build.
- One study is in verification at a time.
- A study enters the hero only after a passing report.
- A study enters the work index only after its case study is complete.
- Every case page uses one layout: heading, about, brief, process, result, credits, gallery.
- Case copy comes from `data/site.json`. `docs/studies/<id>.md` is generated from it.
- A fact that is not in the repo, the project files, or a source we can open is marked unconfirmed.
