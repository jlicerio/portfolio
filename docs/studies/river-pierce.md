# River Pierce Foundation AR Showcase — Case Study

**Brief.** The River Pierce Foundation commissioned an augmented reality project for its 2025 program at the Treviño–Uribe Rancho. The piece debuted on 10 May 2025 as the closing item of The Teaching of the Hands: a free screening of Carolina Caycedo and David de Rozas' documentary, then a panel with Carrizo Comecrudo representatives, then the AR at 3 pm. The subject came from the site. The event page states that the AR project and the food menu both take inspiration from the history of the Carrizo Comecrudo in San Ygnacio.

**Process.**

1. The brief was the place. San Ygnacio sits on the Rio Grande, and the rancho is a historic building held by the foundation and supported by a Mellon Foundation Humanities in Place grant.
2. The program fixed the schedule: screening at 1 pm, panel at 2 pm, AR debut at 3 pm, food by Malaquite at 3 pm.
3. The AR shared the room with a film, a panel, and a food service, so it ran as a one-hour debut rather than an installed work.
4. The event was free and open to the public. That set the access bar: a visitor with no appointment had to be able to take part.
5. The commission put the AR after the testimony rather than before it, so the panel and the film carried the history and the AR closed the day on it.
6. The Laredo Film Society attended with resources on film as practice, which framed the AR as part of a public program and not a product launch.
