# Collapsed Timelines In Felicitous Space — Case Study

**Brief.** Collapsed Timelines In Felicitous Space ran from 4 to 26 March 2022 at the McAllen Creative Incubator, 601 N Main St, McAllen. C. Díaz built the show around California landscapes and geographical memory: the film Sacred Landscapes: The Arroyo Seco, the four-channel video installation Compressed Time, and the painting Body Landscapes No. 1. My part was the technical setup: four channels of playback on monitors, the projection, and the wall text that frames the room.

**Process.**

1. The show carried two video paths at once. One film played on the hanging screen, and Compressed Time ran across four monitors as a set rather than a mirror of one source.
2. Four channels need four sources and four start points, so the monitors were set up as separate players with a sync check before opening.
3. The monitors sit on white cylindrical plinths along the brick wall, with the cable runs on the floor behind them. That keeps the screens at seated eye height and leaves the floor clear.
4. The wall carries a bilingual projection of the film notes in Spanish and English. It is the first text a visitor reads on entering.
5. The projection was aligned to the screen and to the wall, then checked again with the house lights at event level.
6. The show closed on 26 March with a dialogue with sound artist Jon Almaraz and a deep listening session in the same room, so the space had to work as a screening room and as a listening room.
